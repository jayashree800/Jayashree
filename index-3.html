Scene 02 — About

The
story.

Hi, I'm Jayashree. A few months ago I walked into my first B.Tech IT lecture not quite sure what a compiler actually did. Now I can explain it, and I also know how it feels when one politely tells you that you forgot a semicolon.

I like making small things I can show someone: a page, a script, a tiny game. My favourite moment is the one after an hour of being stuck, when it suddenly works and I say “oh” out loud to an empty room. I ask too many questions in class, and I keep a notebook of ideas that mostly begin with “what if”.

This site is my first real project that isn't homework. I built it like the opening of a film because that's how I like to explain things: one scene at a time.
